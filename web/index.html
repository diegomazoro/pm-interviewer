PROBLEM SOLVING

Revolut -- Why are support tickets and response times spiking?

  1. Problem structuring: 4/5 -- Opened with a clear split between demand (ticket volume) and capacity (response time) before proposing anything, rather than jumping straight to a fix.
  2. Clarifying questions: 4/5 -- Asked early whether the spike was app-wide or concentrated in one flow, and whether support headcount had changed.
  3. Quantitative reasoning: 3/5 -- Correctly used the 60% volume increase and 4x response-time growth to size the problem, but didn't sanity-check whether headcount alone could explain the full gap.
  4. Business/commercial sense: 4/5 -- Tied the recommendation to churn risk and public complaints, not just an internal SLA metric.
  5. Insight generation: 3/5 -- Identified the recent app update as the likely driver once the exhibit was requested, but didn't probe further into which specific feature was responsible.
  6. Synthesis & recommendation: 4/5 -- Delivered one clear, prioritized recommendation instead of a list of options with no pick.
  7. Communication: 4/5 -- Signposted clearly ("first demand, then capacity") and built on the interviewer's answers instead of re-asking.
  8. Composure & adaptability: 3/5 -- Adjusted reasonably when told the bug-fix ETA was longer than expected, though took a moment to recover the framework.

TOTAL: 29/40

Frequently asked questions

Is the AI feedback actually good enough to trust?

Each case has its own hand-written rubric, not a generic one -- your transcript is graded across 8 structured dimensions (5 for product-sense cases) with evidence cited from what you actually said, the same way a trained case interviewer would grade you.

What companies do the cases model?

Cases are modeled on the style and substance of real PM interviews at companies like Figma, Stripe, Notion, Anthropic, Mercado Libre, and more. They're original, fictional scenarios written for practice -- not real confidential material -- built to feel like the real thing.

Do I actually need to talk out loud?

Yes -- Loudcase is voice-first on purpose. A real case interview is a live, spoken conversation with follow-ups and pushback, which is a different skill from silently outlining an answer on paper.

What happens after my free interviews run out?

You get 2 full scored interviews for free, with the same detailed feedback Premium gets. After that, Premium ($150 one-time) unlocks unlimited interviews and keeps the detailed feedback going instead of just a score.

Can I retry a case if it doesn't go well?

Yes -- cases can be replayed as many times as you like. Performing differently on a repeat run is part of the practice.

Is my interview data private?

Your transcripts and scores are tied to your account only -- they're never shared publicly or used to identify you elsewhere.
