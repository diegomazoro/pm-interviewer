The best place for PMs to practice live case interviews, out loud

Loudcase runs the kind of live case interview you'll actually face for PM roles at top tech companies -- including product sense, widely considered the hardest PM interview to prepare for. A voice interviewer presents the case, answers your clarifying questions, and never gives you a hint. Talk it through out loud, the way the real interview works, and get a structured scorecard the moment you're done.

Product sense, practiced live

Open-ended design and improvement prompts -- "how would you fix X," "design a feature for Y" -- for companies like Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, Meta, Netflix, and Amazon. The interviewer pushes back with real follow-ups, the same way the actual interview does.

Diagnostic cases too

Metric drops, retention dips, support bottlenecks, fraud spikes, and roadmap trade-offs -- modeled on the case-style questions asked for PM roles at companies like Figma, Notion, Revolut, and Stripe.

No coaching, by design

The interviewer only reveals facts you ask for, in the order the case allows -- it won't nudge you toward the answer, even if you ask directly.

Instant scorecard

Every session is scored on a rubric matched to the case type -- structuring and insight for diagnostic cases, user empathy and product taste for product sense -- with evidence from your own words.

Case scenarios are original and fictional, written for interview practice. Company names are used to reflect the style of real interviews and do not imply affiliation with or endorsement by those companies.
