Company brand icons shown on the case-library cards (practice.html,
company.html). Source: Simple Icons v13.21.0 (https://simpleicons.org),
CC0 licensed; recolored white for the dark UI. The brands themselves remain
trademarks of their owners.

File names are the company slug from catalog.js (e.g. "Mercado Libre" ->
mercado-libre.svg). Companies without a file here fall back to a letter
monogram. To add one, drop a square, single-color white SVG here and add the
slug to COMPANY_ICONS in catalog.js.
