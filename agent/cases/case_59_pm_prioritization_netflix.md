# Case File 59 — Netflix: Growing the Ad-Supported Plan (PM Prioritization Case)

## A. Case prompt (read aloud to candidate at the start — nothing else revealed yet)

"You're a PM on Netflix's advertising product. Next half you can build one of three things: better ad targeting using viewing data, new interactive ad formats, or measurement tools that show advertisers whether their ads led to sales. Your director wants to know which one to build and why. How would you approach this?"

**Objective (private reference, reveal only if asked directly):** Choose the advertising investment that best grows ad revenue, based on what limits revenue today.

---

## B. Clarifying-question answer key

| # | Likely question | Answer | Reveal rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "What's the goal of the ads business this year?" | Grow ad revenue per member on the ad-supported plan. | On request -- key framing fact |
| 2 | "What limits ad revenue today?" | Reveal Exhibit 1 once asked about constraints, sell-through, pricing, or advertiser data. | On request |
| 3 | "What do advertisers say?" | Reveal Exhibit 2 once asked about advertiser feedback. | On request |
| 4 | "How much effort does each take?" | All three are about one half-year of work. | On request |
| 5 | "How do members feel about ads?" | Members accept the current number of ads; increasing ad load is off the table this year. | On request |
| 6 | "What do competitors offer?" | Other streaming services offer similar targeting; some offer interactive formats; a few large ad platforms offer strong sales measurement. | On request |
| 7 | "Are there privacy constraints?" | More detailed targeting with viewing data faces privacy review and member trust concerns. | On request |

**Guardrail reminder:** Do not tell the candidate which project to pick -- let them find the bottleneck in the data.

## D. Model answer / "what good looks like"

**Framework a strong candidate should build:**
1. Anchor on the goal (ad revenue per member) and constraint (no more ad load).
2. Find the bottleneck: is revenue limited by price, demand, or something else?
3. Map each project to the bottleneck and quantify.
4. Recommend, with risks.

**Correct core insight:** Revenue isn't limited by price (similar to competitors) or by ad load (fixed) -- it's limited by demand: 38% of slots are unsold, and 40% of large advertisers who tested didn't come back, mostly (55%) because they couldn't prove results. Measurement directly attacks the bottleneck. Targeting addresses 20% of churned advertisers and carries privacy risk; interactive formats address only 10%.

**Quantitative reasoning a strong candidate shows:**
- Unsold slots worth about $500M a year at current prices.
- If measurement wins back a meaningful share of non-renewing advertisers (55% cite it), it could fill a large part of that gap; targeting and formats address much smaller shares.

**A strong final recommendation includes:**
- Build measurement tools (e.g. partnerships to measure sales lift, privacy-safe methods).
- Note targeting as a later investment with privacy review.
- Metrics: advertiser renewal rate, sell-through, ad revenue per member.

**Scoring anchors:**
- 5/5 business sense: identifies demand/sell-through as the bottleneck, links measurement to non-renewal, quantifies, and addresses privacy.
- 3/5: picks measurement without connecting to the bottleneck, or picks targeting with a reasonable argument.
- 1/5: picks interactive formats or targeting without using the data.
